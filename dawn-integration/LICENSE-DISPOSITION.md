# World Intelligence MCP licence disposition

## Verified repository state

The repository is licensed under the **MIT License**.

## Wave 1 decision

DAWN may retain or adapt MIT-licensed source provided the copyright and licence notice are preserved. This does not grant unrestricted rights to the information returned by third-party data providers.

## Required before enabling a commercial signal source

Record:

1. the source API or website;
2. source terms and permitted business use;
3. licence, attribution and redistribution requirements;
4. API limits and expected cost;
5. data freshness and expiry policy;
6. privacy and personal-data classification;
7. evidence and provenance fields;
8. source-specific disable and rollback steps.

Signals may support research, meeting preparation, account prioritisation or draft outreach angles. They must not automatically trigger trading, contact or other consequential action.

## Merge gate

The restricted commercial-signal schema and offline validator may become technically merge-ready when CI and the Mac pull-and-test pass. Each live data source requires a separate canary and terms review.
